COMPETITOR BUILD WIDTH

Patch 0002 is what makes this host comparable at all: without it upstream
leaves HAVE_ARM_NEON undefined on Apple Silicon and every hash builds without
NEON. With it, XXH3-64 reports impl "neon". gxhash-64 is omitted here: that
port only takes its hardware path under x86 AES, so an ARM figure would
describe SMHasher3's software AES rather than gxhash. See the Zen 5 record.

CORRECTIONS APPLIED

Small-key figures are corrected for SMHasher3's once-per-process overhead
calibration, which ranged 2.26..2.98 cycles here; baseline used 2.26. See the
Zen 5 record for the full explanation. The small-key column is dependent
latency over 1..31-byte keys, not independent throughput.

REPLICATE DISPERSION, AND WHY THE MEDIAN IS STILL SOUND

This fanless host ran for about 70 minutes continuously. One replicate,
mostly replicate 4, came out uniformly fast across nearly every hash at once
(hayahash 27.9 against 35.6..35.8 in the other four). A host-level excursion
affecting all hashes together is consistent with the core clocking above the
rate SMHasher3's cycle timer assumes, not with any property of the hashes.

The median over five replicates is insensitive to it. Dropping the single
furthest replicate per hash gives the dispersion below; the raw max-minus-min
is reported too, because it looks alarming and the reason it does is this
one excursion rather than genuine instability.

CHANGE FROM v0.3.0

Essentially none on this host, despite the dispatch shape flipping from
TIERS=1 to TIERS=0: hayahash moved 33.3 to 33.5 cycles/hash and 9.7 to 9.8
bytes/cycle, both within the dispersion above. That is expected. The tier
switch only affects 17..319-byte keys, of which this column covers 17..31,
and the bulk column hashes 256 KiB where tiers play no part.
