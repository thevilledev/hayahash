SMALL-KEY CORRECTION

1..31-byte average. The calibration varied between 2.72 and 2.92 cycles here.
As documented in docs/smhasher3.md, each reported average was put onto the
minimum-overhead baseline before taking the median:

  corrected = reported average + run overhead - 2.72
