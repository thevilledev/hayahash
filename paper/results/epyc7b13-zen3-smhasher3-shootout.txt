READ THESE FIGURES AS INDICATIVE, NOT AS A CROSS-HOST COMPARISON

This is a shared virtual machine: four vCPUs, no cpufreq control, no
dedicated cores, and a virtualized TSC whose relationship to the actual core
clock is not controlled here. SMHasher3 derives cycles from that counter, so
the absolute bytes-per-cycle and cycles-per-hash values are not comparable
with the bare-metal M1 and Zen 5 records. Ratios between hashes measured on
this host in the same session are the part worth reading.

The scale of the offset is visible in the data: every hash here, including
ones with no relationship to each other, lands roughly 2.4 to 2.9 times lower
in bytes/cycle than on the bare-metal Zen 5 box (XXH3-64 19.1 against 48.9,
rapidhash 10.0 against 28.6, ChibiHash2 6.5 against 15.7). A uniform shift
across unrelated implementations is a property of the measurement setup, not
of the hashes.

Precision is nevertheless good. The overhead calibration was constant at 3.63
cycles across all 50 runs, and after correction the replicate spread is at
most 0.15 cycles on the small-key column. The numbers are repeatable; it is
their calibration against real core cycles that is uncertain.

Small-key figures are corrected for SMHasher3's once-per-process overhead
calibration; baseline used 3.63 cycles. See the Zen 5 shootout record for the
full explanation. The small-key column is dependent latency over 1..31-byte
keys, not independent throughput.

WHAT THIS HOST ADDS

It is the only machine in the set with no AVX-512, so it shows what the
vectorization gate being off costs in practice rather than by simulation.
hayahash's bulk lead over ChibiHash v2 collapses to 1.05x here (6.8 against
6.5), where the same comparison on the vectorized Zen 5 build is 1.99x. The
small-key picture matches the other x86 host: ChibiHash v2 is ahead, 17.1
against 24.6.

hayahash64 across the two shapes this host can reach:

  gcc   (tiers 1, vecgcc 0)   small 24.7 cy   bulk 6.8 B/cy
  clang (tiers 0, vecgcc 0)   small 24.6 cy   bulk 6.3 B/cy

Both are unvectorized, so this pair isolates the tier dispatch rather than
the vectorization, and the two are close.
